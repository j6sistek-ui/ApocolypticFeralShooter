GRAPHICS (ALL RIDES AND WALKING)
D-pad Up / F6: open graphics and pause the game.
D-pad / arrow up-down: choose row; left-right: change the pending option.
A / Enter: apply, save and resume. B / F6: cancel and resume.
Image choices: Native TSR, DLAA, DLSS Quality/Balanced/Performance/Ultra Performance.
Only supported GPU modes appear. DLAA keeps full resolution; DLSS trades resolution for speed.
Frame generation: Off, supported fixed multipliers, or Dynamic. Reflex turns on with FG.
Frame generation preferences are saved in editor Play but apply only in Standalone Game.
NVIDIA suspends generated frames while the game window is in the background.
Use Play > Standalone Game or the supplied Open_AFS_Standalone.cmd launcher for FG testing.
DLAA/DLSS image modes also work in editor Play. No restart is needed to change menu options.

VEXA / GLIDER
On foot: left stick / WASD moves relative to the camera; Vexa turns into travel.
Hold L3 to run: walk 5 m/s, run 9 m/s. While aiming she strafes.
Glider: R2 / E thrust; LS / WASD forward-back climbs/dives, left-right steers.
Right stick / mouse: aim/look only, independent of glider heading and climb.
L1 / Q held while steering increases bank for a tighter turn. Release levels it.
Hold X (0.65 seconds): mount nearby glider/bike/truck, or safely dismount glider.
Glider exit needs clear walkable ground within 1.5 m of the pads and speed below 3 m/s.
The unoccupied glider has gravity and collision; wait for it to settle before mounting.
A / Space: jump on foot; mounted ollie pops up then settles, with a small pulse.
On glider, L3 held / Shift: boost; the meter drains and recharges after a delay.
On foot, hold B / Shift for the Parkour template's crouch/slide; release to stand.
F7 / D-pad Right while grounded toggles CLS poses and the original locomotion.
Both use the same Parkour movement, wall jump, glider, guns and vehicles.
Release glider thrust to coast to a stop. B / Ctrl: brief coast assist.
L2 held / right mouse: first-person ADS; release restores the selected view.
On foot R2 / left mouse fires. On glider R1 / left mouse fires; R2 is thrust.
Unscoped third-person fire raises the visible gun. The Ballista has a centred reflex sight.
Three body hits or one headshot kills a zombie.
Zombies respawn to maintain 20; blood impacts and ragdoll deaths show hits/kills.
Ammo is unlimited. Vexa is invulnerable, and zombies have no attack damage.
F5 / Menu: toggle FPS and external review camera.
R: reset Vexa and glider to the test start.

SUPPLIED HOVERBIKE CONTROLS
Controller: RT accelerate; LT brake, then continue holding to reverse.
Left stick left/right: steer. B: supplied boost. Right stick: camera.
Keyboard: WASD / arrows retain throttle/reverse/steering, Space handbrake,
Shift boost and F lights.
Mouse / right stick: camera. Mouse wheel: zoom. V: change camera.
E or hold X: exit and return to Vexa. Exit clears throttle, steering and boost,
and applies parking brakes and drag; re-entering releases parking controls.

DOOMSDAY TRUCK (SUPPLIED CONTROLS)
R2 / W / Up: throttle. L2 / S / Down: brake and reverse.
Left stick / A-D: steering. L1 / Space: handbrake.
Right stick / mouse: look. View / Tab: toggle camera. Menu / Backspace: reset truck.
Hold Y (controller) or hold X (keyboard): exit and return to Vexa.
The vendor's controller X throttle and A brake bindings remain intact.
