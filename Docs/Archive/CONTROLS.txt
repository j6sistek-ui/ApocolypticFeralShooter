AFS FREE FLIGHT TEST

TEST SETTINGS (ALL RIDES AND WALKING)
D-pad Up / F6: open graphics/level selection and pause the game.
L1 / R1 / Tab: switch to Zombie Settings in Cyber City or Lakeside.
Zombie health and speed apply to living enemies; wave size changes next round.
Spawn interval: larger means slower spawning. These adjustments last for this session.
D-pad / arrow up-down: choose row; left-right: change the pending option.
A / Enter: apply, save and resume. B / F6: cancel and resume.
Test level: Post-apocalypse, Cyber City, Chinese Lakeside Town or NYC - Exploration.
NYC has Vexa, hoverboard and Doomsday truck, with no zombies. Other levels retain all three vehicles.
Applying a different level starts a fresh session; current positions/kills are not carried over.
Invincible: On by default; turn Off to test Vexa's health. At zero health she
resets at the level start after3seconds. A saves; B cancels. Vehicle damage is unchanged.
Lakeside uses its own Horror, BogZombie, Mythical, Plant and Pirate creatures.
It has separate Zombie Engine waves/ammo/inventory and never uses the Cyber City roster.
Image choices: Native TSR, DLAA, DLSS Quality/Balanced/Performance/Ultra Performance.
Only supported GPU modes appear. DLAA keeps full resolution; DLSS trades resolution for speed.
Frame generation: Off, supported fixed multipliers, or Dynamic. Reflex turns on with FG.
Frame generation preferences are saved in editor Play but apply only in Standalone Game.
NVIDIA suspends generated frames while the game window is in the background.
Use Play > Standalone Game or the supplied Open_AFS_Standalone.cmd launcher for FG testing.
DLAA/DLSS image modes also work in editor Play. No restart is needed to change menu options.

VEXA / GLIDER
On foot: left stick / WASD moves relative to the camera; Vexa turns into travel.
Hold L3 to run: walk 5 m/s, run 9 m/s. While aiming she strafes.
Glider: R2 / E thrust; LS / WASD forward-back climbs/dives, left-right steers.
Right stick / mouse: aim/look offset, independent of glider heading and climb.
The camera follows glider turns while retaining that look offset.
L1 / Q held while steering increases bank for a tighter turn. Release levels it.
Hold X (0.65 seconds): mount nearby glider/bike/truck, or safely dismount glider.
Glider exit needs clear walkable ground within 1.5 m of the pads and speed below 3 m/s.
The unoccupied glider has gravity and collision; wait for it to settle before mounting.
A / Space: jump on foot; mounted ollie pops up then settles, with a small pulse.
On glider, L3 held / Shift: boost; the meter drains and recharges after a delay.
On foot, tap B / Shift while moving to slide; hold for a forward dive into prone.
While stationary, tap crouches/stands and hold goes prone. A stands up from prone first.
Standing checks headroom. Capsule floor contact stays fixed.
F7 / D-pad Right while grounded toggles CLS poses and the original locomotion.
Both use the same Parkour movement, wall jump, glider, guns and vehicles.
Release glider thrust to coast to a stop. B / Ctrl: brief coast assist.
L2 held / right mouse: gun sights in FPS, external screen zoom in third person.
On foot R2 / left mouse fires. On glider R1 / left mouse fires; R2 is thrust.
On foot tap R3 / V quickly slashes with the katana; A remains jump.
Hold R3 for0.45seconds to equip the katana. R2 / left mouse swings while equipped.
Hold R3 again to return to the gun. Switching while holding R2 requires releasing
the trigger before using the new weapon. The katana uses no ammo; reload is inactive.
The rifle hides while swinging or while the katana is equipped. Melee is unavailable
while riding, knocked down, defeated or in inventory. Mounting returns to gun selection.
Unscoped third-person fire raises the visible gun. The Ballista has a centred reflex sight.
PostApo: three body hits or one headshot kills a zombie; population replenishes to20.
Cyber City: Zombie Engine waves grow in health/count/speed. Four standard appearances,
brute rounds12/14/16... and eight-kid swarm rounds5/15/25... are editable defaults.
Cyber City and Lakeside headshots use each weapon's damage multiplier against
the target's scaled health; they are not forced instant kills. The brute's landing stomp knocks nearby
on-foot Vexa down within4m; walls block it. The top-right panel shows wave progress.
Walk/drive over dropped pickups: Nuke clears live zombies; Speed lasts15seconds.
PostApo ammo is unlimited. Cyber City and Lakeside use magazine/reserve ammo and reloads.
Vexa starts invincible; the pause-menu option allows normal health loss.
Occupied vehicles remain invulnerable. City zombies play pursuit/run,
attacks, hit reactions and deaths; PostApo remains a replenishing target range.
F5 / Menu: toggle FPS and external review camera.
Home: reset Vexa and glider to the test start. R also resets in PostApo.

CYBER CITY COMBAT / INVENTORY
R3 does not jump or roll; it uses the common on-foot katana action above.
Tap X / R: reload. Hold X / F on a focused supply crate or loose item: interact.
Hold X near a ride still mounts it. Reload is cancelled when mounting or using melee.
Three street supply crates offer Ballista tiers for500/1250/2500 gold; an owned
weapon's crate sells60 reserve rounds for250 gold. Starting gold500, magazine30,
reserve120. Kills pay the configured zombie reward. Weapon swaps retain spent ammo.
D-pad Left: cycle equipped quickbar weapons. Keyboard1..5: select a quickbar slot.
D-pad Down / Tab: inventory. B / Escape: close. Left stick moves its cursor;
A clicks or holds to drag. Mouse drag/drop uses the supplied inventory UI.
The native inventory provides item slots, quickbar, weight and equipment capacity.
ZESP live settings: editor Window > ZESP Control Panel while Play is running.
See Docs/ASSET_AUDIT_REPAIRS_20261025.txt for current supported fields and limits.
The controller Zombie Settings page changes four session fields; it is separate
from the full editor-only RGCL panel. Wave damage scaling and the active map's
starting-equipment settings are now used. Ballista tiers and katana are the
configured loadouts; installing another weapon does not fit its model to Vexa.

SUPPLIED HOVERBIKE CONTROLS
Controller: RT accelerate; LT brake, then continue holding to reverse.
Left stick left/right: steer. B: supplied boost. Right stick: camera.
Keyboard: WASD / arrows retain throttle/reverse/steering, Space handbrake,
Shift boost and F lights.
Mouse / right stick: camera. Mouse wheel: zoom. V: change camera.
E or hold X: exit and return to Vexa. Exit clears throttle, steering and boost,
and applies parking brakes and drag; re-entering releases parking controls.

DOOMSDAY TRUCK (SUPPLIED CONTROLS)
R2 / W / Up: throttle. L2 / S / Down: brake and reverse.
Left stick / A-D: steering. L1 / Space: handbrake.
Right stick / mouse: look. View / Tab: toggle camera. Menu / Backspace: reset truck.
Hold Y (controller) or hold X (keyboard): exit and return to Vexa.
The vendor's controller X throttle and A brake bindings remain intact.

Deferred: target locking, multiplayer, production tuning and main-game integration.
Dedicated human slide and prone-entry/exit clips remain an asset gap. The local
slide is a grounded leg/torso variant of the owned crouch pose; crawl now uses
the owner's supplied crawl animation, retargeted without its donor mesh. Controller cursor feel
and final visual motion need owner review.
The dive uses the existing prone pose in flight; dedicated dive transition animation remains an asset gap.
FPS now uses Vexa-derived arms with the purchased idle/reload animation and a
stable sight anchor. Mounted reload layers the CLS action above the glider pose.
The static Ballista has no detachable magazine, so the hand animation is still
an approximation of a physical reload. The full Vexa body is shown in F5 review.
Directional glider jets attach to existing board features. Rear jets follow
thrust; lift, descent, side and braking jets respond to flight demand/motion.
No nozzle geometry was added. Hold-X assisted landing remains a planned feature.
