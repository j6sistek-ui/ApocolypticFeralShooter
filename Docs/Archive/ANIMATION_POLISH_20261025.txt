DIVE / PRONE SUPPORT
The landed dive now stays in its existing belly sequence for a 0.45-second
settle. Restarting the same sequence on landing previously blended through
the upright CLS pose and lifted the torso; that restart is removed. Different
posture clips use matching blend-in/out times. Resting and armed prone use
the purchased belly pose; moving unarmed crawl uses the owner's supplied loop.
Hands can remain on the rifle while the forearms support the upper body.

Two-bone contact correction preserves limb lengths and the authored bend side.
It corrects foot penetration during posture blends and brings forearms and
feet/toes to a supported resting pose. Unreachable contacts use the closest
possible bend rather than stretching a limb. The gaze is raised from the
source zombie crawl's floor-facing head pose, and the rifle sits beside it.
The standing rifle torso overlay no longer lifts a prone character's elbows.
Rifle finger posing and support-hand contact remain active while prone aiming.

Rendered flat-floor checks: elbow joint centres 5-7.83cm above the floor in
four resting/aiming samples, toe centres 2.28-3.49cm. These heights account for
limb thickness; joint centres are not the visible skin surface. Both forearms
and feet provide the intended support, with a slightly higher support elbow
when the rifle reaches forward. The landed-dive interval peaks at 4.97cm
elbow-centre height. No interim upright torso spike remains in that capture.
Minimum foot/toe clearance during the full transition is 1.34cm; camera-arm
distance changes by 0cm. These are scripted inputs and sampled rendered poses.
Uneven ground, stairs and extreme prone aim angles remain unvalidated. Contact
correction currently uses the floor height beneath the capsule, not separate
terrain traces under every limb. Dedicated human dive/prone entry/exit clips
remain an asset gap; this is an adaptation of available motion, not new mocap.

KATANA

GLB SHA256: 3e77f001234d80c1fdfa3deff6e4b75ff0bf3c95d963754bea17593ee17a5852.

The source GLB and original geometry remain the reimport authority; do not use
the historical filename-guessing exporter to recreate these texture bindings.
