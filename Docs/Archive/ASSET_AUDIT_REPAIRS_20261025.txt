AFS ASSET AUDIT REPAIRS — 2026-10-25

This supersedes the October 25 read-only audit's implementation status. It does
not replace that audit's baseline evidence or claim full purchased-pack coverage.
Local sandbox only. This pass did not edit supplied source projects or owner
source art; AFS.uproject and ten latest kid-source files passed hash checks.
No GitHub publication or SpaceSurvival integration.

COMBAT AND MOVEMENT
The active ZESP weapon owns ammo, spread, range, damage and firing cadence. The
bridge now honors its hitscan/projectile choice, projectile speed/gravity/class,
headshot multiplier, surface effects and shell fallback. The five authored
Ballista tiers explicitly use hitscan. Their brief tracer is presentation, not
a second damage projectile. Normal headshots no longer force an instant kill;
wave attack damage scaling is applied. Invincibility remains on by default.
Each map reads its own starting equipment. Current supported equipment is the
Ballista tiers plus katana. Arbitrary installed guns require presentation setup.
Advanced Shooter remains installed but unused; ZESP is the single combat owner.

CLS now has local rifle profile/chooser/database copies using 22 existing clips,
with native rifle lower-body movement and ADS upper-body contact. Available
directional hit reactions and slow crouch entry/exit are used. Parkour continues
to own movement and wall actions. No replacement pawn or second movement owner.
The owner's supplied crawl animation was retargeted to Quinn/Vexa; its donor
geometry was not imported. Moving crawl loops; idle freezes; stand checks room.
Tap B while moving slides, hold B dives into prone. Dedicated human dive/prone
entry/exit and wall-run animation remain asset gaps; the pack has no hidden
skeletal animations to enable. Functional actions do not imply finished clips.

FIRST-PERSON AND RIDING PRESENTATION
Vexa-derived view arms use purchased idle/reload animation and the native reload
timer. Their bounds now cover the view model so they render consistently. The
gun follows the animated hand and retains a stable sight anchor. View arms hide
in third person. Glider reload layers the existing CLS action above the riding
pose with pad contact preserved. The Ballista is a static mesh without a separate
magazine; the borrowed reload hand path remains approximate. A matched physical
magazine exchange is not delivered by this pass.
The new FPS arms cover the rifle; katana retains its prior presentation and
still needs a dedicated first-person hand/weapon animation for equal polish.
Per-shot controller feedback now comes only from confirmed shots. Motor feel
and audio quality still need physical controller/listening checks.
Bike rider body processing is re-enabled after the vendor disabled it. Body and
chain motion use attached vehicle velocity; accepted on-foot spring tuning stays.

GLIDER
Controls are unchanged: R2 thrust; LS pitch/steer; RS look/aim; L1 stronger bank;
R1 fire; A ollie; held L3 boost. Right-stick camera input no longer leaks into
rider steering. Separate pitch poses, six movement/turn start/end clips, nine
aim offsets and aim entry/exit use the purchased glider pack. Feet remain locked.
The white/blue directional jet candidate is now the saved booster reference.
Its source Niagara solver was disabled; the local copy now simulates positions,
with corrected size/alpha curves, short lifetime and restrained emission. Rear,
lift, descent, side and brake demand follow controls and motion, including light
hover support. Effects attach to existing board features; no nozzle geometry.
Assisted landing remains a separate planned feature, not current hold-X behavior.

ZOMBIES
Each Lakeside definition retains its correct native retargeter and now has four
attack choices and four hit reactions. Purchased Attack_A/C are prone attacks
and excluded from standing encounters. Attack_B has measured forward strikes at
.95 and 2.35 seconds; Attack_D at .5 seconds. Original clips stay untouched.
Cyber City's standard/brute/kid roles and Lakeside's five-creature exclusion rule
remain separate. No suitable creature vocal sounds were established from the
audited libraries; silence is not filled with unrelated effects.
All 11 custom meshes now point to reversible four-LOD copies. LOD0 is preserved;
lower levels target 55%, 25%, 12% triangles, with original UV/material references.
Close and attacking enemies retain full pose updates; distant enemies may use
engine update-rate/visibility budgeting. This is not a large-horde FPS benchmark.
Texture edits to shared assets propagate. After a geometry reimport, regenerate
the optimized copy from its original mesh; do not reimport into the reduced LODs.
